# Support — Equipped Workshop

Equipped Workshop is the companion app for the Equipped workshop at
Cumberland Trace church of Christ in Bowling Green, Kentucky. It has the
schedule, the speakers, the building map, food trucks and vendors, and
My Schedule: your own list of sessions, with a reminder before each one.

## Getting help

- **Email:** [KingCreationsSupport@protonmail.com](mailto:KingCreationsSupport@protonmail.com)
- **Testing the app through TestFlight?** Open the TestFlight app, choose
  Equipped Workshop, and tap **Send Beta Feedback**. A screenshot of what
  you're looking at helps.

Questions about the workshop itself (registration, lodging, what is being
taught and when) belong with the church:
[ctchurchofchrist.com/equipped](https://www.ctchurchofchrist.com/equipped).
The app can only show what the workshop has announced.

## Common questions

**Do I need an account?**
No. There's no sign-up and no login.

**How do I build My Schedule?**
Open a session and tap its star, or swipe left on a session in the
Schedule. Starred sessions are listed by day on the **My Schedule** tab,
and two that overlap are marked.

**How do reminders work?**
The first time you star a session, the app asks whether you want a
reminder before each one. The bell on My Schedule sets how long before,
or turns reminders off. If nothing arrives, check that notifications are
allowed for Equipped in your device's Settings. Reminders are for the
upcoming workshop, not for past years.

**The schedule looks out of date.**
The app checks for a newer schedule when you open it, at most once an
hour. To check right now, pull down on the Schedule. The bottom of the
**Info** tab shows which schedule you have and when the app last checked.

**Does it work without a connection?**
Yes. The schedule, speakers and building map are kept on your device.
Only recordings and other links need the internet.

**Where are earlier years?**
Use the year button at the top of a tab to choose a year. The Speakers
tab can also show every year at once, and a search of the Schedule can
cover all years. Sessions from past years link to their recordings on
YouTube and the podcast, where there is one.

**My starred sessions aren't on my other device.**
Each device keeps its own My Schedule; there is no account to carry it
from one to another. The exception is Apple Watch, which shows the stars
from your iPhone.

**What does the Apple Watch app show?**
What is on now and next, your My Schedule, and the day's schedule. You
star sessions on your iPhone.

**Can I share my schedule?**
Yes. The share button on My Schedule makes a picture of it that you can
send or save.

**Which devices does it run on?**
iPhone and iPad with iOS 26 or later, and Apple Watch with watchOS 26 or
later.

**What about my privacy?**
The app has no accounts and collects nothing about you itself. The
[privacy policy](privacy) has the details.
