# Privacy Policy — Equipped Workshop

*Effective October 8, 2026.*

Equipped Workshop ("Equipped") is the companion app for the Equipped workshop at Cumberland Trace church of Christ in Bowling Green, Kentucky: the schedule, speakers, building map and your own list of sessions. The shortest honest summary: **the app has no accounts, we run no servers, and the app itself collects nothing about you.** One feature involves someone else. If you choose to watch a recording inside the app, it plays in YouTube's player, and Google receives what any YouTube player receives. That is explained below.

## What the app stores, and where

Everything you set in the app is kept **on your own device**: the sessions you star for My Schedule, when you want to be reminded, and a few small settings. The app also keeps a copy of the schedule files it last downloaded, so it opens without waiting on the internet.

**Your starred sessions follow you between your own devices through iCloud**, if your device is signed in to iCloud. The app hands the list to Apple's iCloud key-value storage and Apple carries it to your other iPhones, iPads and Macs that run the app under the same Apple Account. We never see it: the list goes to your iCloud, not to us, and the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) governs it there. With iCloud off, each device simply keeps its own list.

If you have an Apple Watch, your iPhone passes your starred sessions to your own watch so the watch can show them, using Apple's connection between the two.

None of this is sent to us. Like any app's data, it is part of your device's backup if you back up your device, and Apple's privacy protections govern that.

## What the app sends over the internet

**Schedule files.** At launch, and when you return to the app, it checks **public files hosted in this repository** for an updated schedule, so a change can reach you without an app update. This is an ordinary file download. It sends nothing about you: no account, no identifier, nothing you have starred. GitHub, which hosts the files, sees the request the way any web host does, including your IP address; the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) covers that. If the download fails, the app quietly uses what it already has.

**Recordings, if you choose to watch one in the app.** Sessions from past years have recordings on YouTube. Each one offers two choices: **Watch Here**, which plays it inside the app, and **Watch on YouTube**, which leaves the app. Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you press Watch Here.

- Watch Here plays the recording in YouTube's embedded player. To do this the app uses **YouTube API Services**. By using this feature you agree to be bound by the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms).
- When the player loads, **Google** receives information directly from your device, as it does from any YouTube player: for example your IP address, details about your device, which recording is played, and how you use the player. Google's use of that information is described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://www.google.com/policies/privacy).
- The player is content served by a third party. YouTube may show advertisements in it, and it may store and read information on your device, such as cookies or similar technology, in order to work.
- The app limits this where it can. It asks for YouTube's privacy-enhanced player, in which Google says a view is [not used to personalize YouTube or advertising](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/171780). You are not signed in to a Google account in the app, and the app discards the player's cookies and stored data when the player closes.
- The app itself does not record what you watch, and nothing about it is sent to us. There is no Google sign-in in the app, so there is no access for you to revoke.

Watch on YouTube, and the app's other links (Maps, email, phone, the podcast, the workshop's website), open in other apps or your browser. What happens there is covered by those services' own policies.

There are no analytics, no advertising of ours, no tracking, no third-party SDKs and no crash reporters in the app.

## Notifications

If you allow them, the app reminds you before the sessions in My Schedule. The reminders are created on your own device; nothing is sent to us or anyone else to make them. You can change the timing or turn them off in the app, or turn notifications off in your device's Settings.

## Photos

You can share My Schedule as a picture. The app makes the picture on your device and hands it to the share sheet. If you choose Save Image, your device saves it to your photo library. The app never reads your photos.

## Children

The app is for everyone who attends the workshop. We collect nothing from anyone, adult or child. Watching a recording in the app involves Google as described above.

## App Store privacy label

**Data Not Linked to You: Usage Data.** This describes the YouTube player. When you watch a recording in the app, Google counts the view. Nothing else is collected, by us or anyone else, and nothing is used to track you.

## Changes

If a future version of the app changes any of this, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be visible in this repository's public edit history.
